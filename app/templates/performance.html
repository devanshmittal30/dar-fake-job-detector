Model Performance & Metrics

How well does DAR work?

Full transparency on model evaluation, training datasets, confusion matrices, and decision thresholds.

Why accuracy alone is misleading: Only about 5% of ads in the training data are fake, so accuracy alone can look high. Precision and recall show how well fake ads are caught.

Evaluation Metrics on 3,150 Test Ads

Evaluated on a 20% stratified test split that the models never saw during training.
