Protect yourself from fraudulent recruiters and fee traps.

Rule 1 of 10 Use arrow keys or buttons to navigate

Real employers never ask for money

Registration, training, kit or security deposit fees are a scam sign.

If You Have Been Scammed

Take immediate action to minimize financial loss and report the fraudsters:

1

Stop paying immediately

Never pay any additional "clearance tax", "wallet unlocking fee", or "refund processing charge". Any further payment is lost.

2

Save screenshots and receipts

Capture complete WhatsApp/Telegram chat histories, phone numbers, UPI handles, transaction IDs, payment confirmations, and the original ad poster.

3

Call National Cyber Helpline 1930

Dial 1930 right away. The Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting System operates 24/7 to freeze funds in transit during the golden hour.

4

File a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in

Lodge an official FIR complaint on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in).

5

Tell your bank immediately

Inform your bank's fraud division to dispute the unauthorized transaction, block compromised debit/credit cards, and reset UPI PINs.
